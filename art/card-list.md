# Card art list

All 40 cards, grouped by color. Save each image as `art/cards/<file name>.webp`, then run `npm run art`.

**Spec for every image:** 4:5 portrait (800×1000), no text or frame, subject centered (the game draws number tablets on the middle of each edge and a border around it).

**Shared style:** hyper-realistic 2D dark fantasy painting in the spirit of Gwent card art. A dark, dangerous jungle: humid mist, overgrown ruins, shafts of light through the canopy. Dramatic chiaroscuro, painterly detail, one focal character or creature. Matte and gritty, not glossy.

**Color accent:** the game themes each card by its color, so let the painting's accent light follow it:
- **Green:** deep moss and canopy greens
- **Red:** firelight, embers, dried blood
- **Blue:** cold river teal, mist, slate stone

Rarity can show in the scale and drama of the scene: commons are small everyday dangers, legendaries are ancient, huge, or divine.
