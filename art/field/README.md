Art for the 4×4 board, its special squares, and the rule twists. Send PNG masters; they get converted to WebP for the game.

## Rules for every asset

- Same gritty, painterly dark-fantasy style as the card art.
- Straight top-down view for the board, blocked squares, and table.
- No text, UI, or frames.
- Twist overlays sit over the cards: keep the middle 70% nearly clear (under ~20% opacity).
- Animated extras (embers, moving water, swaying leaves) are added by the game on top.
