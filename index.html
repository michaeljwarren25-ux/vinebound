    How to play
    
    
      
      
    1. Each side brings 8 cards. You hold 3 at a time and draw a new one each time you play. Your opponent's hand stays hidden, and a coin toss decides who goes first.
      2. 
      
    2. Take turns placing a card on the 5×5 board. Stone squares are blocked.
      3. 
      
    3. Every card has an Attack number, a Defense number, and arrows (from 1 to 8, corners included) showing where it attacks. When your card points at an enemy card, and that card points back, it's a battle: your Attack against its Defense. Higher wins; a tie holds. If it doesn't point back, you take it for free.
      4. 
      
    4. Every card you capture attacks again along its own arrows, so one play can set off a chain. The whole chain is settled with the numbers showing when your card landed, so what you see is what fights.
      5. 
      
    5. Watch the attack itself: claws rake out of your card along the arrow they use. An attack that gets through bursts and turns the card over; one that fails breaks on the shield the defender puts up.
      6. 
      
    6. ☀︎ Sunlit squares give +1 to Attack and Defense. ❦︎ Undergrowth squares give −1. A = 10.
      7. 
      
    7. Every card is Green, Red or Blue. A colored line on one side of its frame means it wants a card of that color there; a dashed line all around means anywhere next to it. On the board, a card's attack arrows are colored to show who holds it: green arrows are yours, red arrows are your opponent's. The frame keeps the card's own color, so a card looks the same on the board as it does in your hand.
      8. 
      
    8. When it gets that color, the card has Advantage and its ability works: stronger numbers, weaker enemies, boosts for corners, edges, or the center. Any card of that color counts — yours or your opponent's. Ownership never matters, only the color.
      9. 
      
    9. Hover a card (or press and hold on a phone) to read its ability. After you pick a card, squares where it would get Advantage are marked, and hovering a square shows what you'd capture there.
      10. 
      
    10. When every card has been played, whoever holds more cards wins. Equal is a draw.
      11. 
      
    11. Build a 14-card deck in your Collection. Each Quick Match deals you 8 of them. You start with a themed pack, and the other packs are on sale in the Collection.
      12. 
      
    12. Every deck carries one Rite: a power you may use once a match, on your turn, free — it doesn't cost you the card you play that turn. Your opponent brings one too, and you can see which. Rites stow a card you can't use, raise your numbers, or reshape the board: open a square to the sun, bury one under stone, clear the stone off another, or pull one of your own cards back off the board. Three come with your starter pack; the rest are unlocked with amber, and each does something the others don't. Equip one to each deck in the Collection.
      13. 
      
    13. Win to earn amber and trade it for new cards. Play the Daily Duel several days in a row for rare card rewards.
      14. 
      
    14. Walk the Jungle Trail for tougher opponents who bend the rules, and earn Trophies along the way.
      15. 
      
    15. The Trail uses its own campaign deck. Campaign cards start weaker, earn XP every time you win there, and gain a point each level: spend it to raise Attack or Defense by 1. Quick Match, the Daily Duel and challenges always use fixed numbers.
